The world

Shabrān, the kingdom of night

Shabrān

A distant kingdom

Its name means “night” in Persian. Noisy markets, a great square, a sand arena, a palace of dark galleries.

Two strata

High Nobility, Common Folk

One owns the land, the other works without ever amassing anything. Between them, a wall you cannot see, but that everyone knows.

The market

The heart of the Common Folk

Leaning stalls, voices calling to one another, stories of the city that was. This is where Shaaban spends his days.

A king

An iron hand

His justice is swift, never unjust. His children see him more than they hear him.

Neighbors

Sarhad, Kardun, Nakhur

Three lands, tributes, fragile alliances and long grudges. For now, the peace holds.

The Shadow

One voice, two eras

The story is told from Babylon, years later, with the melancholy of those who remember.
